SCIENCE DEEP DIVE

The Case For New Molecular Chemistry

THE PROBLEM WITH EXISTING DRUGS

  • Psilocybin: off-patent, 4–6 hour duration, non-selective binding, zero IP protection for developers
  • Ketamine: dissociative, significant abuse potential, NMDA mechanism not ideal for all patient populations
  • SSRIs: delayed onset of weeks, 30% non-response rate, same core mechanism unchanged since 1987
  • Every major competitor (CMPS, CYBN, MNMD) is building on off-patent chemistry they cannot exclusively protect

THE AZA APPROACH

  • Aza-substitution = nitrogen atoms replace carbon at key positions in the psilocin indole ring
  • Creates a novel, patentable molecular entity — structurally distinct from everything in the prior art
  • Potentially: shorter duration of action (30–90 min vs. 4–6 hrs), improved therapeutic window
  • Transdermal delivery potential — removes clinic-only administration barrier
  • Active API synthesis at Applied Pharmaceutical Innovation (Alberta) — $146K program underway

THE OPPORTUNITY

  • First — and currently only — company with USPTO-protected aza-psilocin IP
  • No direct competitor holds this compound class. It is not off-patent. It is not in the public domain.
  • Novel chemistry → novel patent → novel commercial exclusivity through ~2041
  • Compass, Cybin, MindMed all using off-patent chemistry subject to generic competition post-exclusivity
  • Eli Lilly acquiring ATAI demonstrates Big Pharma urgency to secure proprietary psychedelic IP
