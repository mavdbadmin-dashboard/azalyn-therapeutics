About

A New Chapter in
Neuropsychiatry

Azalyn Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage neuropsychiatry biopharmaceutical company pioneering a novel class of psychedelic-derived compounds designed to address the unmet medical needs of the most treatment-resistant patients.

Our proprietary AZA Platform is built on aza-substitution chemistry — a structural innovation that creates first-in-class psilocin analogs with tunable pharmacokinetics and differentiated receptor binding profiles. These compounds are not found in nature and are fully protected by USPTO-issued intellectual property.

Founded by a team of drug development veterans and neuropsychiatry specialists, Azalyn is advancing multiple development programs targeting treatment-resistant depression, PTSD, anxiety disorders, and substance use disorders.

The Platform

The AZA Platform

A radical chemical innovation in psychedelic medicine — building the first IP-protected class of aza-substituted psilocin analogs for precision neuropsychiatry.

🔬
Novel Chemistry

Aza-substitution replaces a carbon atom in the indole ring system with nitrogen, creating structurally distinct psilocin analogs not found in nature. Patent-protected. First in class. No prior art.

🎯
Precision Binding

Modified 5-HT2A receptor engagement with tunable pharmacokinetics. Dimethylamine and diisopropylamine substituents enable drug design flexibility, creating a robust pipeline of differentiated candidates from a single chemical scaffold.

💊
Clinical Potential

Designed for treatment-resistant depression, PTSD, and anxiety disorders — patient populations with the most significant unmet need and greatest therapeutic opportunity. Pre-IND stage. API synthesis underway with Applied Pharmaceutical Innovation.
