SCIENCE DEEP DIVE

Three Key Scientific Advantages of AZA Chemistry

METABOLIC STABILITY

Aza-substitution replaces carbon atoms in the psilocin indole ring with nitrogen — a modification that materially improves metabolic stability. The nitrogen atom creates an electron-deficient ring system that resists CYP enzyme breakdown, potentially extending the compound's duration in the body and enabling non-IV administration routes. Natural psilocin is rapidly metabolized. AZA-psilocin may not be.

RECEPTOR SELECTIVITY

The R2 diisopropylamine substituent — the specific modification USPTO allowed as novel — creates a bulkier binding profile at the 5-HT2A receptor. This steric hindrance may enable more selective receptor engagement with reduced off-target binding. Reduced 5-HT2B engagement in particular is a key safety consideration in serotonergic drug development. The USPTO specifically found this substituent to distinguish Azalyn's compounds from all prior art.

DELIVERY INNOVATION

Families 2 and 3 of Azalyn's patent portfolio cover carbonate/carbamate prodrug formulations of psilocin — the same chemistry validated by independent ACS journal publications in 2024 and 2026. These prodrug strategies could enable transdermal patches, sublingual delivery, or controlled-release oral formulations. The $800M/year SPRAVATO franchise (J&J esketamine) exists entirely because of a delivery innovation. Azalyn's prodrug patents target the same opportunity.

The First Patented Aza-Modified DMT in the Scientific Literature

THE DMT OPPORTUNITY

N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) is among the most potent and fast-acting psychedelic molecules known. Clinical interest has exploded: GH Research's Phase 2b trial of inhaled 5-MeO-DMT achieved −15.5 MADRS points in treatment-resistant depression — one of the strongest signals in the field. Cybin Inc. (NYSE: CYBN, ~$998M market cap) has built an entire program around CYB004, its deuterated DMT analog. The molecule works. The clinical validation is real. The problem is delivery.

WHY NATURAL DMT FAILS CLINICALLY

  • •Duration: 5–15 minutes (IV). Too short for therapeutic integration.
  • •Administration: Requires IV infusion in monitored setting. Limits accessibility.
  • •IP: Natural DMT is off-patent. No commercial exclusivity possible.

AZALYN'S AZA-DMT ADVANTAGE

Azalyn holds U.S. Patent Application 63/923,194 — 'AZA N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT) Derivatives and Therapeutic Uses Thereof.' This is the first patented aza-modified DMT in the scientific literature. Zero published research on 'aza-DMT' exists — this is genuinely uncharted chemical territory.

The aza-modification may enable:

Modified duration of action

Potential oral/sublingual bioavailability
Novel patent-protected exclusivity

If confirmed in preclinical studies, Azalyn's AZA-DMT could represent a clinically viable, patent-protected DMT — something neither natural DMT nor Cybin's deuterated approach can offer.
